Privacy policy

Summary

This site uses no cookies, includes no analytics and has no forms. Fonts are served from this domain, and reading the site loads nothing from other servers. The one exception is the model checker: when you use it, your browser contacts Hugging Face directly, as explained below.

Data controller

Carlos Díaz, email modelpoisoning@gmail.com.

What data is processed

Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When a GitHub Pages site is visited, GitHub logs the visitor’s IP address for security purposes (GitHub Docs). This is processed on the basis of legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) to keep the site secure and working. GitHub handles this data under its own privacy statement, and states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for transfers outside the European Union.

Model checker. The checker runs in your browser. When you press “Check”, your browser sends the name of the model to Hugging Face’s public API (huggingface.co), which receives your IP address as it would with any visit to its website. This site does not receive or store the query. Hugging Face handles that data under its own privacy policy. If you do not use the checker, your browser makes no request to Hugging Face.

Emails. If you write to modelpoisoning@gmail.com, your address and message are processed only to reply to you, on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), and kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry.

This site does not share data with third parties unless required by law.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to modelpoisoning@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Changes

If cookies or analytics tools are added in future, this policy will be updated and, where the law requires it, your consent will be requested before they are used.
